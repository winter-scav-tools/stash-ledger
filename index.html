How to read this page Keep counts are exact per upgrade and quest, so they drop as you tick. Crossed-out entries are uses you've finished. Ticks are saved in this browser only. To move them to another device, use "Back up or move progress" in the Innards log. Per space is credits per unit of rig space, for deciding what to drop when your rig is full. Large-bin items have their own slot. Not listed: weapons, ammo, gun attachments (sell any you don't run), and your own kit like med kits, grenades, turrets and rig lights. Prices can drift after patches, so trust the in-game vendor number.
